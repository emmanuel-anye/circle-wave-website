# Business information needed

The Phase 1 components intentionally avoid unverified claims. Supply and approve
the following before enabling richer conversion content:

## Trust material

- Testimonials: exact approved wording, full attribution, role, organization,
  and written permission to publish.
- Client proof: approved organization name, logo asset, alt text, and written
  permission for public logo use.
- Outcomes or case studies: verified result, measurement method, timeframe,
  source owner, supporting evidence, and approved public wording.
- Any staffing scale, geography, response-time, placement-rate, or service-level
  claim: a current source and owner who approves publication.

## Consultation booking

- The approved public HTTPS booking URL.
- Meeting owner, meeting name, duration, availability rules, and fallback
  contact owner.
- Confirmation that the booking service's privacy notice and data handling are
  acceptable.

## Analytics

- Selected analytics provider and account owner.
- Consent requirements and approved cookie/consent language, if applicable.
- Retention period, data residency, access roles, deletion process, and approved
  privacy-policy updates.
- Approved conversion goals and reporting owner.

Until this material is supplied, trust content stays hidden, the consultation
CTA falls back to the contact form, and analytics events have no active
provider.
